## Summary

Figure posted video of its Figure 03 humanoid climbing a short ladder/stair structure autonomously (claimed, not independently verified), attributed to the on-robot Helix VLA system rather than teleoperation or a scripted routine. The robot is shown repeatedly scanning for handholds and pulling itself up, illustrating continuous balance adjustment and coordinated arm-leg control — a capability generally considered difficult for bipedal humanoids.

## Key Contributions

- A public demonstration of whole-body loco-manipulation combining dynamic balance, handhold search, and coordinated limb control on a commercial humanoid platform.

## Strengths

- Tackles a genuinely hard mobility case (ladder/vertical climbing) that most humanoid demos avoid.

## Weaknesses

- No technical writeup, success-rate data, or methodology has been disclosed — this is marketing content only.
- Not independently verified.
- Too thin on technical substance to assess rigorously; may be a cherry-picked take from many attempts.
